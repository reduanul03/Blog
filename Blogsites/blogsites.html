Receiving the first prize of team Cloud

We are proud to share that our team from the University of Information Technology and Sciences (UITS) secured 2nd prize in a competitive programming contest where 30 groups participated. The event showcased talent, teamwork, and problem-solving under pressure. It was a challenging contest that tested our coding knowledge, logic-building skills, and our ability to work together efficiently within a limited time. Throughout the competition we faced several difficult problems, but with strong communication, strategic thinking, and determination, we managed to solve them effectively. Our team’s coordination and hard work played a big role in our success. We were honored to receive the award from the Dean of UITS and the Head of our department in a proud and memorable prize-giving ceremony. Their encouragement and recognition mean a lot to us. This achievement motivates us to aim higher sharpen our skills further and continue representing UITS with excellence in future competitions.

25th Nov, 2024

Got certificate from NASA API challenge

We are proud to share that we participated in the NASA API Challenge from Bangladesh. It was an exciting opportunity where we worked on real-world space and data-related problems using NASA's open APIs. This challenge helped us learn new skills, improve our creativity, and work as a strong team. After completing the challenge successfully, we were honored to receive certificates in recognition of our efforts. The certificates were handed over to us by our respected Al-Imtiaz sir (Department Head of CSE) and Md. Safaet Hossain sir (Director, IQAC) which made the moment even more special. Their appreciation and support encouraged us a lot. We are thankful for the opportunity and proud to represent our institution in such an international event. This experience has inspired us to take part in more global competitions and keep learning.

19 Feb, 2025

Receiving 1st prize of Postal Persentation

We are very happy to share that we won 1st prize in the Poster Presentation competition held at the University of Information Technology and Sciences (UITS). It was a proud moment for our team as we worked hard on our project named Cloud Drone designed a creative and informative poster and presented it with confidence. Many talented teams participated which made the competition challenging and exciting. Our efforts were recognized and rewarded during the prize-giving ceremony. We were honored to receive the award and certificate from our respected Dr. Md. Ashraful Islam Sir (Dean, Faculty of Science & Engineering), Al-Imtiaz Sir (Department Head Of CSE), and Md. Safaet Hossain Sir (Director, IQAC). Their presence and kind words made the moment even more special for us. This achievement motivates us to keep learning and improving our skills. We are thankful to our teachers and the university for their support and for giving us this platform to showcase our ideas. We feel proud to represent UITS and aim for more success in the future.

Got 4th prize in Bangladesh Youth Development

We are proud to announce that our team Cloud Drone secured 4th prize among 55 teams in the Bangladesh Youth Development competition. It was a national-level event where young minds from across the country showcased their innovative ideas and talents. Competing with so many skilled and creative teams was both challenging and inspiring. We worked hard on our project to focusing on solving real-world problems with practical solutions and our efforts paid off. Receiving 4th place in such a competitive environment is a great achievement for us and it has motivated us to aim even higher. The experience helped us grow our skills in teamwork, problem-solving and presentation. We are thankful to the organizers of Bangladesh Youth Development for creating such a wonderful platform for the youth. This recognition encourages us to continue our journey of learning and innovation and we look forward to taking part in more competitions and making a positive impact in the future.
